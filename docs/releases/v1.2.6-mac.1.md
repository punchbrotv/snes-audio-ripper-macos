# Music first, sound effects optional

New extractions export music only by default. Enable **Include sound effects (SFX)** before opening a ROM to include effects in MP3, WAV, MIDI, or Logic Pro exports. The checkbox starts off each time the app launches and is locked while extraction runs.

Music discovery and track numbering stay unchanged. The manifest identifies excluded effects, and music-only extraction never falls back to raw sound-effect files. Command-line users can opt in with `--include-sfx`.
