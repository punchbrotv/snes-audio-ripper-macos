# Instrument icons, rainbow colors, and phrase regions

Logic exports now open with instrument icons matched to their descriptions and distinct region colors flowing through the rainbow in track order. Track icons share their region’s color. Unknown descriptions use Logic’s Sampler icon.

The appearance is embedded in every new project. Notes, controllers, beat alignment, samples, and routing are preserved. No Apple artwork, game-specific names, profiles, ROMs, or extracted samples are bundled.

Rests longer than one full measure now separate phrases into individual regions. The project meter determines the threshold; sustained and overlapping notes remain together. Region edges follow the notes, and each phrase keeps its original timing, controllers, instrument, name, and color.
