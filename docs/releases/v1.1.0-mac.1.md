# SNES Audio Ripper for macOS — v1.1.0-mac.1

Choose **MP3, WAV, or MIDI** in the new **EXPORT AS** selector before opening a ROM. The app remembers your choice.

- **MP3:** compact audio, with built-in playback.
- **WAV:** lossless 16-bit PCM audio, with built-in playback.
- **MIDI (experimental):** editable notes in eight voice tracks, with note timing, velocity, pan, and pitch bends. Click a file to open it in your MIDI app.
- The command-line tool accepts `mp3`, `wav`, or `midi` as its final argument.

MIDI traces commands from the game's sound program. Sample tuning is estimated; uncertain samples use a placeholder root note. Assign instruments in your DAW. Original samples, DSP effects, and envelopes are not included, and sounds without recoverable note events are skipped. Use WAV or MP3 when you want the rendered audio.

Download **SNES-Audio-Ripper-macOS-arm64.zip**, unzip it, and move the app to Applications. Requires macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon. The download includes the runtime, native emulator core, and audio dependencies. No source gathering or separate tools are needed to run it.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. If macOS blocks opening it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after attempting to launch it, if you trust this release. `SHA256SUMS.txt` provides the download checksum.
