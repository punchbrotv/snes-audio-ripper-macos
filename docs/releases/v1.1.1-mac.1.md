# SNES Audio Ripper for macOS — v1.1.1-mac.1

Fixes missing music banks and inconsistent track discovery between export formats.

- Discovers ROM upload blocks using their correspondence with captured sound memory, including small drivers and short block headers.
- Finds song banks and verifies their command ports and pointer-table bounds by observing the sound CPU's reads. No title-specific profiles or ROM identifiers were added.
- Uses repeatable controller input and scans all four command ports in the snapshot fallback.
- Completes discovery before WAV, MP3, or MIDI encoding. Export errors no longer interrupt discovery or renumber later tracks.
- Writes `manifest.json` with the discovered track list, stable track IDs, exported files, and individual failures. Partial exports are identified in the app.

MIDI remains experimental: tuning is estimated, instruments must be assigned in your DAW, and original samples, envelopes, and DSP effects are not included.

Download **SNES-Audio-Ripper-macOS-arm64.zip**, unzip it, and replace the previous app. Requires macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon. The runtime and extraction dependencies are included.

The app is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. `SHA256SUMS.txt` provides the download checksum.
